About

World-class digital humanities under a foundation's roof

T-Corpus is a digital humanities center established within the Türkiye İmam Hatipliler Foundation (TIMAV). It aims to be the first center in Turkey producing open, reusable and versioned research data for Islamic studies.

Why T-Corpus?

Turkey's major digital platforms offer search interfaces — but no Islamic studies corpus with downloadable datasets, an API, DOI-versioned releases and an open license yet exists. T-Corpus was founded to fill that gap.

The three-layer principle

The shared secret of international exemplars (OpenITI, KITAB, CLARIN) is a three-layer stack: structured data, rich discovery interfaces, and open tools. Every T-Corpus project builds all three together.

The TIMAV legacy

Active in education since 1994, TIMAV brings 13 workshop reports, 25 books and three decades of institutional experience as the ground of the center's research agenda.

Methods

Four principles

1

Source fidelity

Every record is tied to its source; numbers are generated from code, never typed by hand, and are reproducible.

2

Openness

Datasets are published under open licenses, versioned, and wherever possible with a DOI.

3

Human review

Borderline decisions are never auto-resolved; they are flagged for expert judgment.

4

Honest status

Beta is beta, draft is draft. Unfinished work is not presented as finished; past projects are archived, not erased.
